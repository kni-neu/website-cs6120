# Syllabus: CS 6120 - Natural Language Processing

[View Official PDF Syllabus](pdfs/nlp_syllabus.pdf)

## Overview
This course in Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a mix of first principles and heavy doses of software engineering in the project setting. The key objectives are two-fold: (1) teach fundamental concepts of NLP and (2) to provide extensive and practical hands-on modeling experience. Our language modeling curriculum will cover a variety of use cases, including but not limited to sentiment analysis, question / answer, summarization, translation, and more. The core topics to be covered in this course include topic models, word/sentence embedding models, deep attention models, and large language models (LLMs).

## Objectives

By the end of this class, students will have first-hand knowledge and be able to replicate (if not contribute to) to modern NLP approaches. They will be able to read most of the technical papers that advance this technology. They will have a track record brought about through practical deployment of [projects](#/assignments/final-project) and [labs](#/assignments). But perhaps most importantly, I hope they will have a rewarding experience that they can leverage in the future.

## Grading Policies
Grading is based on a mix of individual and group efforts across projects and participation:

| Item | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Participation & Attendance | 10% |
| Labs | 10% |
| Reading Groups & Quizzes | 15% |
| Final Project | 15% |
| Assignments | 20% |
| Final Exam | 30% |

Labs must be turned in by the following lecture. Late assignments will have 3 point deductions each weekday until the following lecture. You must notify instructional staff (via e-mail) ahead of any absences.
