# Reading Group Guidelines

Keynote papers are required reading that underly the fundamentals of modern natural language processing theory and practice. Each week, students should sign up for a paper. There are two deliveries: a video and a writeup. Links to both should be added to the [spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16UzbPkleajr5yHGeqTyXksXyUPIyQEum2jZKbhIM2U4) on the due date listed in the syllabus.
