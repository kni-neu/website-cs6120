# Reading Group Guidelines

Presentation slots are associated with weekly topics. Please ensure your chosen paper aligns with the week's theme or consult with the instructors for approval of alternative papers.

Presentation materials (slides/notes) should be added to the [spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16UzbPkleajr5yHGeqTyXksXyUPIyQEum2jZKbhIM2U4) (there is a lag between when you update and the below viewport).
